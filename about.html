How We Began

A Vision Birthed in Prayer

Love of Christ Generation Church (C&S) was founded on a divine mandate — to raise a generation that fully embodies the love of Christ and carries the glory of God to every nation, tribe, and tongue.

Born from a heart of prayer and a deep passion for souls, this ministry has grown from humble beginnings into a thriving global community with campuses in the heart of London, England, and Victoria Island, Lagos, Nigeria.

The church is proudly part of the Eternal Sacred Order of the Cherubim and Seraphim — one of Africa's most historic Pentecostal Christian movements — and carries its rich heritage of prayer, worship, and holiness into the modern generation.

Every Sunday, hundreds of worshippers gather to encounter God through powerful praise, anointed preaching, and heartfelt prayer. The ministry extends beyond its walls through social media, reaching over 231,000 people on Instagram alone.

Our Founder

Rev. Mother Esther Abimbola Ajayi

Globally revered as "Iya Aduraa" — meaning "The Woman of Prayer" — Rev. Mother Esther Abimbola Ajayi is one of the most respected and beloved spiritual leaders of her generation. Her life is a testament to the faithfulness of God and the transforming power of persistent prayer.

A visionary, philanthropist, and devoted servant of God, Rev. Mother Ajayi founded Love of Christ Generation Church with a singular purpose: to raise a generation that knows God intimately and demonstrates His love boldly. Under her leadership, the church has grown from a small prayer group into a thriving, international ministry with thousands of faithful members across the United Kingdom and Nigeria.

Her ministry is marked by extraordinary signs and wonders, a deep commitment to social justice, and a compassion for the poor that has led to numerous philanthropic initiatives across Nigeria and beyond. She is known for her free garment distributions, feeding programs, and her tireless advocacy for those who are marginalised and forgotten.

Rev. Mother Ajayi is a proud member of the Eternal Sacred Order of the Cherubim and Seraphim — a movement she has faithfully served and represented with honour, leading the church to celebrate historic milestones including its 30th Thanksgiving Anniversary.

Our Heritage

Cherubim & Seraphim — C&S

Love of Christ Generation Church is a proud member of the Eternal Sacred Order of the Cherubim and Seraphim — one of the most influential African Christian movements in history.

Founded in Nigeria in 1925, the Cherubim and Seraphim movement is renowned for its emphasis on prayer, the power of the Holy Spirit, healing, and vibrant, heartfelt worship. It carries a rich tradition of spiritual depth, cultural expression, and communal faith that has blessed millions across Africa and the diaspora.

At LOC Generation Church, we honour this heritage while embracing the needs and expressions of today's generation — creating a space where the ancient fire of the Spirit meets the boldness and creativity of the present.
