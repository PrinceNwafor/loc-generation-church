Generosity

Your Giving
Builds God's Kingdom

Every seed you sow is an act of worship. Your generosity funds the Gospel, blesses the poor, and transforms lives for eternity.

Why We Give

Giving is an Act of
Faith and Worship

The Bible says, "Bring the whole tithe into the storehouse, that there may be food in my house. Test me in this... and see if I will not throw open the floodgates of heaven and pour out so much blessing that there will not be room enough to store it." (Malachi 3:10)

At LOC Generation Church, giving is not a burden — it is an honour. When you give, you partner with God in funding His work: feeding the hungry, spreading the Gospel, training the next generation of believers, and covering those in need.

Every pound and naira given here is handled with integrity and accountability, directed toward the mission God has placed on this church.
