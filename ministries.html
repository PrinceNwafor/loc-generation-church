Ministries for
Every Generation

Whatever your age, background, or calling — there is a place for you to serve, grow, and make an eternal difference at LOC Generation Church.

Serving Together

Find Your Ministry

Every member of the body of Christ has a gift and a call. Discover the ministry where God's Spirit is leading you.

🌟

Children's Church — Little Stars

A vibrant, age-appropriate church experience for children aged 0–12. Our dedicated team creates a safe, fun, and spiritually enriching environment where the next generation falls in love with Jesus. Runs concurrently with Sunday services.

Get Involved
🔥

LOC Youth Ministry

For teenagers and young adults aged 13–30. Through dynamic worship, relevant teaching, and authentic community, LOC Youth exists to help the next generation discover their identity in Christ and live boldly for God in their schools, universities, and workplaces.

Join Youth
👩

Women's Fellowship — Daughters of Zion

A community of women united in faith, prayer, and fellowship. The Daughters of Zion meet monthly for worship, the Word, and mutual support — equipping women to thrive in every role: wife, mother, professional, and daughter of the Most High God.

Join Fellowship
👨

Men's Fellowship — Men of Valour

Men of Valour is a brotherhood of men committed to following Christ with integrity, leading their families with love, and serving their communities with courage. Monthly gatherings, accountability groups, and outreach programmes.

Join Brotherhood
🎶

Praise & Worship Team

Our anointed team of singers and musicians lead the congregation into God's presence every Sunday. If you have a gift for music or singing and a heart for worship, we'd love for you to serve alongside us and help usher others into an encounter with God.

Audition
🙏

Prayer Ministry — The Watchmen

Prayer is the engine of everything we do. The Watchmen are a dedicated team of intercessors who cover the church, its leaders, members, and community in fervent prayer. From early morning prayer to night vigils — they stand in the gap for every soul.

Pray with Us
🌍

Evangelism & Outreach

Driven by the Great Commission, our evangelism team takes the love of Christ into streets, hospitals, prisons, and communities. We run feeding programmes, visitation missions, and open-air gospel events — because the Gospel is for everyone, everywhere.

Serve Outreach
📱

Media & Digital Ministry

With over 231K Instagram followers, our media team amplifies the Gospel online. From live streaming services to producing sermon content, social media management, and website ministry — this team ensures the Word reaches the digital generation.

Join the Team
💛

Welfare & Compassion Ministry

Inspired by Rev. Mother Esther's heart for the poor, our welfare team coordinates food banks, clothing drives, and financial support for those in need — both within our congregation and in the wider community. Because love must be seen in action.
