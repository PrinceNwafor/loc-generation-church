Statement of Faith

What We Believe

Our beliefs are rooted in the Holy Scriptures and the rich heritage of the Cherubim and Seraphim movement — timeless truths for every generation.

At Love of Christ Generation Church C&S, our faith is anchored in the Holy Bible as the inspired and infallible Word of God. The following represents the core beliefs that form the foundation of our life together as a community of faith.

01

The Holy Trinity

We believe in one God, eternally existing in three persons: God the Father, God the Son (Jesus Christ), and God the Holy Spirit. These three are co-equal, co-eternal, and one in nature, essence, and purpose. Every act of salvation, sanctification, and service flows from the Triune God.

02

The Holy Scriptures

We believe that the Bible — both Old and New Testaments — is the inspired, inerrant, and authoritative Word of God (2 Timothy 3:16). It is the complete and sufficient guide for all matters of faith, doctrine, and Christian living. We stand on the Word and the Word alone.

03

The Lord Jesus Christ

We believe in the deity and humanity of Jesus Christ — fully God and fully man — conceived of the Holy Spirit, born of the Virgin Mary. He lived a sinless life, died on the cross as the atoning sacrifice for our sins, was bodily resurrected on the third day, ascended to the Father, and will return again in glory.

04

Salvation by Grace Through Faith

We believe that salvation is a gift from God, received through faith in Jesus Christ alone (Ephesians 2:8-9). It cannot be earned by works or merit. Repentance from sin and confession of faith in Christ as Lord and Saviour is the doorway to eternal life and a restored relationship with God.

05

The Holy Spirit & Spiritual Gifts

We believe in the Holy Spirit, the third person of the Trinity, who convicts the world of sin and empowers believers for holy living and effective witness. We affirm the baptism of the Holy Spirit with evidence of speaking in tongues, and we embrace the full operation of spiritual gifts (1 Corinthians 12) in the life of the church today.

06

The Power of Prayer

We believe prayer is the lifeblood of the believer and the church. As a Cherubim and Seraphim church, prayer is central to all that we do. We believe God hears and answers the prayers of His people (Jeremiah 33:3), and that fervent, persistent prayer moves mountains and changes destinies.

07

Healing & Miracles

We believe that Jesus Christ is the same yesterday, today, and forever (Hebrews 13:8). The God who healed the sick, raised the dead, and performed miracles in the Bible still performs signs and wonders today. We believe in divine healing for spirit, soul, and body through faith in the name of Jesus.

08

The Church & Community

We believe in the Church as the body of Christ — a community of believers called to worship God, build one another up, care for the poor, and share the Gospel with the world. The local church is God's primary vehicle for transformation in society, and membership in it is essential to the Christian life.

09

The Second Coming of Christ

We believe in the personal, visible, and glorious return of Jesus Christ. He will come again to judge the living and the dead, establish His eternal Kingdom, and make all things new. This blessed hope purifies us and motivates our mission to reach the lost while there is still time.

10

Resurrection & Eternal Life

We believe in the bodily resurrection of the dead. Those who have trusted in Christ will be raised to eternal life in the presence of God. Those who have rejected Christ will face eternal separation from God. It is our great commission and privilege to ensure that as many people as possible receive the gift of eternal life.

"Jesus said to him, 'I am the way, and the truth, and the life.
No one comes to the Father except through me.'"

John 14:6
