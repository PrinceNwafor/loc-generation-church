Our Heart

A Church That Shines
with God's Glory

Love of Christ Generation Church is more than a congregation — it is a movement of God's love, reaching across London, Lagos, and the world.

Founded and led by Rev. Mother Esther Abimbola Ajayi, our church stands on the bedrock of prayer, the Word, and genuine love for every soul. Whether you are searching, hurting, or simply hungry for God — you have found your home.

Our Spiritual Leader

Rev. Mother Esther
Abimbola Ajayi

Known globally as "Iya Aduraa" — the Woman of Prayer — Rev. Mother Esther Abimbola Ajayi is a visionary leader, philanthropist, and servant of God whose ministry has touched countless lives across continents.

Her unwavering faith, compassion for the poor, and powerful prayer ministry have made her one of the most beloved spiritual figures in the Cherubim & Seraphim movement worldwide.
