When You're Hurting

God is near to the broken-hearted. You don't have to go through this alone.

Pain is real. Heartbreak is real. Grief is real. And God — who sees every tear — is closer to you right now than you know. The Bible is filled with people who were broken, who cried out, who questioned — and found that God never abandoned them. He will not abandon you either.

"He heals the brokenhearted and binds up their wounds."

Psalm 147:3

Where Do I Start?

I've lost someone I love
I'm struggling with illness
I'm dealing with rejection
I'm going through betrayal
My marriage is in pain
I'm experiencing financial hardship
I feel emotionally broken
Something else is hurting me

"Heavenly Father, I come to You with a heavy heart. You see my pain — every tear, every sleepless night, every broken moment. Your Word says You are close to the broken-hearted. Draw close to me now. Bring healing to every wound I carry. Replace my mourning with joy and my heaviness with praise. I trust You, Lord — even when I don't understand. In Jesus' name, Amen."

A Message for You

"God Will Heal Your Broken Heart"
Rev. Mother Esther Ajayi

Remember — pain has a season. What you're feeling now is not your forever. God is working in the middle of your mess, turning what the enemy meant for harm into your testimony of His faithfulness. Hold on. The glory that comes after this will be worth it.
