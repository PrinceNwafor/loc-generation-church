Your First Visit

What to Expect

We want your first visit to be as smooth and welcoming as possible. Here's a simple guide to help you feel prepared.

🚗
Arrive & Park

Plan to arrive 10–15 minutes early. In London, we're on Clapham Road (SW9 9DA), near Stockwell tube station. In Lagos, we're at Water Corporation Drive, Victoria Island. Street parking is available near both sites.

🤝
Be Greeted

Our Welcome Team will meet you at the door with a warm smile. Let them know it's your first time — they'll help you find your seat, answer questions, and introduce you to others. You are never alone here.

👶
Drop Off Children

We have Little Stars, our dedicated children's programme, for ages 0–12. Our trained carers will look after your little ones with age-appropriate Bible lessons and activities during the main service.

🎵
Experience Worship

Our services begin with powerful, Spirit-filled praise and worship — a hallmark of C&S worship culture. Come with an open heart and expect an encounter with the living God through music, prayer, and the Word.

📖
Hear the Word

Every service features a powerful message grounded in Scripture and relevant to your life today. You can follow along in your own Bible or use the screens in the auditorium — we'll display all passages clearly.

☕
Stay & Connect

After the service, stay for refreshments and fellowship. Pick up our welcome pack, meet the pastoral team, and learn about our ministries. We'd love to hear your story and introduce you to yours here at LOC.

🙏

"Wherever you are coming from, whatever you are carrying — you are welcome here. At LOC Generation Church, we believe that every person who walks through our doors is a divine appointment. God has already been expecting you."

Rev. Mother Esther Abimbola Ajayi
Founder & Senior Pastor, Love of Christ Generation Church C&S
Common Questions

First-Time FAQs

Come as you are! We warmly welcome everyone regardless of what they wear. Many of our members dress smartly, but there is no dress code. As a C&S church, some members choose to wear white garments as a sign of purity and reverence — you are in no way expected to do so as a first-time visitor. Just come comfortably and with an open heart.

London: Street parking is available on Clapham Road and surrounding streets. We're also very accessible by public transport — the church is a short walk from Stockwell station (Victoria & Northern Line). Buses 88, 155, and 345 stop nearby.

Lagos: Parking is available on-site at Water Corporation Drive, Victoria Island. Please allow extra time on Sunday mornings.
Our Sunday services typically last 1.5–2 hours and include: vibrant praise & worship with our talented music team, prayer (a hallmark of C&S tradition), announcements, a Scripture-based message from our pastor or a guest minister, an altar call for those wishing to receive prayer, and a time of fellowship afterward. Services are conducted in English.
Yes! Our Little Stars children's ministry serves children aged 0–12 during the main Sunday service. Children are welcome to join the main service for the worship portion and are then led to their dedicated space for age-appropriate Bible lessons, crafts, and activities. Our team is DBS-checked (UK) and trained in child safeguarding.
As a first-time visitor, there is absolutely no expectation for you to give. Offering time is part of our regular service, and we believe giving is an act of worship for members who choose to participate — but it is never a pressure. Please focus on your experience of the service. You can learn more about giving on our Give page when you're ready.
